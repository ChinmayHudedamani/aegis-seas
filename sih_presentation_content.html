SLIDE 2: [TEAM NAME] IDEA TITLE SOLUTION & UNIQUENESS
Proposed Solution
  • Autonomous End-to-End Pipeline: Real-time ingestion of Sentinel-1 C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and optical EO imagery, processing raw microwave backscatter to detect, characterize, and isolate oil slick geometries.
  • Physics-Informed Dual Drift Engine: Mackay chemical evaporation kinetics bounding physical slick age ($t_{\text{age}}$), driving an inverted and forward Runge-Kutta 4th-Order (RK4) Lagrangian model (5,000 stochastic parcels) for origin hindcasting and spread forecasting.
  • Adversarial AIS Attribution: Spatio-temporal corridor intersection scoring suspect vessels on proximity, course collinearity, speed-drop anomalies (pumping speed 6–8 knots), and transponder blackout gaps.
Uniqueness & Architectural Differentiators
  • $O(1)$ Native C++20 Core: Clutter background calculated via 2D Summed-Area Tables (Integral Images), achieving sub-second detection without heavy cloud infrastructure or nested-loop bottlenecks.
  • Dark-Vessel Kinematic Inversion: Radon transform wake extraction ($\theta = 19.47^\circ$) and Doppler centroid azimuth shifts ($\Delta y$) identifying vessels that deliberately disable Class-A AIS transponders.
  • UNCLOS Art. 217 Admissibility: Automated Bonn Agreement volumetric integration ($Q > 30\text{ L/NM}$) and SHA-256/ECDSA cryptographic sealing for maritime court enforcement dossiers.
Key Slide Takeaway: Unlike competitor projects relying on cloud-dependent black-box models, AEGIS-SEAS executes sub-second physics and hydrodynamic math directly on air-gapped patrol vessels to identify evasive polluters.
